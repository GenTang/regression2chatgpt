## Overview

This chapter discusses logistic regression. The model has a profound influence on every aspect of daily life, from the advertisements shown on a web page to economic policies formulated at the national level, such as poverty-alleviation programs.

Logistic regression is not only widely used but also a milestone model. Its modeling process embodies an extremely important idea: decompose a problem into a visible layer and an invisible layer, then combine a nonlinear transformation with a linear model to reduce an unfamiliar, complex problem to a familiar, simple one. It is no exaggeration to say that understanding logistic regression in depth means grasping the essence of model building.

The logistic regression model itself has several important characteristics:

* **Simple model:** The modeling approach is clear and easy to understand and master.
* **Broad applicability:** Its assumptions are relatively easy to satisfy, so it applies to a wide range of scenarios. It is used extensively in fields such as medicine, finance, and marketing.
* **Strong interpretability:** Its parameters have direct practical meanings, and their values directly reflect the importance of individual features. This makes it easier to understand how features affect predictions.
* **Reliable results:** Like linear regression, logistic regression has powerful analytical tools for evaluating the stability of model parameters and results. Such analysis improves confidence in the results.
